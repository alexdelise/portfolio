Optimized COVID-19 patient transfers in Florida using a MINLP epidemic model with travel dynamics. Used Florida Department of Health data to minimize unmet hospital demand. Visualized results with matplotlib in Python; solved model using GAMS. Results showed significantly reduced total infections and unmet demand through strategic patient reallocation.
