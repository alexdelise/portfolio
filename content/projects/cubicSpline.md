This study explores cubic spline interpolation methods to approximate polynomial functions, comparing the accuracy of natural and clamped cubic splines. The analysis demonstrates that clamped splines yield more accurate results due to additional derivative information, with both methods' errors verified against theoretical bounds.

See GitHub for source code, and the external link for the report.
