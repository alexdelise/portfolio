Hello! I am Alex DeLise — an undergraduate researcher passionate about optimization, data, and using mathematics to solve real-world problems.

I am currently pursuing a B.S. in Applied and Computational Mathematics and Computational Science at Florida State University, where I am a [Presidential Scholar](https://presidentialscholars.fsu.edu/) and serve as Vice President of the [Pi Mu Epsilon](https://pme-math.org/what-is-pme) Mathematics Honor Society. My academic and research interests lie at the intersection of operations research, industrial engineering, and quantum computing.

I have worked on projects involving mixed-integer linear and nonlinear programming to address healthcare needs in Florida, and even in quantum algorithms, where I have been developing a new framework for quantum constrained optimization for QAOA.

Outside of class, I am involved in mentoring and leadership, and I enjoy stating active.
